Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and session information needed to keep you signed in.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain your Spendly account.
  • Display your expenses, summaries, and spending breakdowns.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • Contact you about important changes to your account or this policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be permanently removed.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers. We may rely on trusted third-party providers, such as hosting services or font providers, to operate the app. These providers may process limited technical data on our behalf and are expected to protect it in line with this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to, or deletion of, your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
